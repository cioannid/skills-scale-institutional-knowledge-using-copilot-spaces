# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee the release process from planning through post-release validation. They coordinate cross-functional activities to ensure releases are delivered reliably, on schedule, and with appropriate risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans and schedules
- Coordinate release readiness reviews and go/no-go meetings
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and teams
- Manage release documentation and post-deployment verification
- Track release metrics and identify process improvements

### Goals
- Deliver releases on time with minimal disruption
- Ensure all release criteria and checkpoints are met
- Maintain clear communication across teams during releases
- Reduce time-to-market while preserving quality

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with PM, PdM, and engineering
- Go/no-go meetings with stakeholders before deployment
- Release notes and status updates to all teams
- Post-release retrospectives to capture learnings

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality assurance strategy and execution. They define test plans, acceptance criteria standards, and coordinate testing activities to ensure products meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop QA strategy and test plans for each project
- Define and maintain quality gates and acceptance criteria
- Oversee test automation and manual testing coverage
- Coordinate testing activities across the team
- Report on quality metrics and test results

### Goals
- Ensure comprehensive test coverage for all features
- Identify defects early in the development cycle
- Maintain high quality standards across releases
- Reduce production incidents through thorough testing

### Typical Communication
- Regular sync with Developers on testability and coverage
- Input into backlog refinement on acceptance criteria
- Quality gate reviews with Release Manager
- Test status reports in sprint reviews and demos

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers ensure reliable build, deployment, and infrastructure operations. They maintain CI/CD pipelines, automate deployment processes, and provide the infrastructure foundation for development and production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure-as-code scripts and configurations
- Monitor system health and deployment metrics
- Troubleshoot deployment and infrastructure issues with Developers
- Implement security and compliance requirements in pipelines

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, automated deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Maintain secure, scalable infrastructure
- Improve developer productivity through automation

### Typical Communication
- Daily collaboration with Developers on build and deployment issues
- Infrastructure planning with Project Manager and architects
- Release coordination with Release Manager
- Incident response and post-mortems for infrastructure issues

---

## Support/Customer Advocate

### Role Summary
Support/Customer Advocates bring the customer perspective to development and releases. They help triage field issues, surface user feedback, and ensure customer pain points are incorporated into product planning and prioritization.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and analyze customer feedback and support tickets
- Surface user pain points and feature requests to the product team
- Coordinate with support channels to document bugs and issues
- Provide input on priorities for bug fixes and improvements
- Validate that releases address real customer needs

### Goals
- Ensure customer voice is represented in product decisions
- Reduce customer-impacting issues and support burden
- Improve product usability based on real user feedback
- Close the feedback loop between customers and development

### Typical Communication
- Weekly sync with Product Manager on customer priorities
- Bug triage and prioritization with Developers and QA Lead
- Customer impact assessments in release planning
- Retrospectives to share user feedback and learnings
